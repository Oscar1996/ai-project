# Cadence Glossary

The shared language for Cadence, a platform where learners buy and take video courses.

## Refunds

**Refund**:
A learner's request to give back a purchase in exchange for the amount paid, together with its whole lifecycle from Requested to Approved or Declined. A Declined refund is still a refund.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel my purchase, reversal, chargeback

**Refund status**:
Where a refund stands: **Requested** (submitted, awaiting review), **Approved** (the amount paid is returned and the learner loses access to the course), or **Declined** (nothing is returned and access is kept).
_Avoid_: Pending, open, in progress

**Refund window**:
The 14 calendar days after a purchase during which a learner may request a refund.
_Avoid_: Cancellation window, cancel window

**Refund eligibility**:
Whether a learner may request a refund on a purchase: it must be inside the refund window, have under 2 hours of the course watched, have under half of the course's lessons completed, and not be a claimed team seat.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer, refundable

> In this workspace, "chargeback" is never used for a refund. A chargeback is a dispute the learner raises through their bank, outside Cadence.
