# Cadence Glossary

The shared language for Cadence, a platform where learners buy and take video courses.

## Refunds

**Refund**:
A learner's request to give back a purchase in exchange for the amount paid, together with its whole lifecycle from Requested to Approved or Declined. A Declined refund is still a refund.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel my purchase, reversal, chargeback

**Refund status**:
Where a refund stands: **Requested** (submitted, awaiting review), **Approved** (the amount paid is returned and the learner loses access to the course and all of their course progress), or **Declined** (nothing is returned and access is kept).
_Avoid_: Pending, open, in progress

**Refund window**:
The 14 calendar days after a purchase during which a learner may request a refund.
_Avoid_: Cancellation window, cancel window

**Refund eligibility**:
Whether a learner may request a refund on a purchase: it must be inside the refund window, have under 2 hours of the course watched, have under half of the course's lessons completed, and not be a claimed team seat.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer, refundable

**Refund amount**:
What an approved refund returns: exactly what the learner paid, never the course's list price. A learner who paid a regional discount price is refunded that discounted price.
_Avoid_: Refundable amount, list price refund

**Refund review**:
A platform admin's decision on a Requested refund: approve or decline. It is a judgment call, not an automatic check; the platform admin may approve a refund the learner no longer qualifies for.
_Avoid_: Moderation, processing

**Course progress**:
Everything a learner has done in a course: lessons completed, video watched, and quiz attempts. Lost in full when a refund is Approved, so a learner who buys the course again starts from scratch.
_Avoid_: Learner data, history

**Post-request activity**:
Learning done on a course after the learner submitted a refund request, shown to the platform admin separately from the progress at the time of the request.
_Avoid_: Extra progress, new progress

## People

**Platform admin**:
A Cadence staff member with authority over the whole platform, including refund review. Not tied to any team or course.
_Avoid_: Admin (unqualified), superuser

**Team admin**:
A member who manages a team's seats and licences. Has no authority over refunds or the platform.
_Avoid_: Admin (unqualified), team owner

> In this workspace, "chargeback" is never used for a refund. A chargeback is a dispute the learner raises through their bank, outside Cadence.
